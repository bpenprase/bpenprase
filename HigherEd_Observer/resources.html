Resources & Reports

The reference shelf of the Observatory: the most relevant long-form reports on the founding of new universities worldwide — from the World Bank, the British Council, UNESCO, the branch-campus trackers, and the accreditors — each with a short overview and a link to the full document.

11 reports on the shelf · scanned pages are re-swept every two weeks

World Bank · 2021 · PDF

Steering Tertiary Education: Toward Resilient Systems that Deliver for All

The World Bank's framework paper on tertiary education - the five 'STEER' principles that guide the largest financier of higher education worldwide. The best single statement of how the Bank thinks about building, diversifying, and sustaining university systems.

British Council · 2024 · PDF

A Global Framework for Transnational Education Engagement

Developed with the QAA and Education Insight, this framework analyzes national environments for transnational education and proposes a consistent language and data foundation for comparing TNE across countries - including the new Indian regulatory environment.

British Council & DAAD · PDF

Transnational Education: A Classification Framework and Data Collection Guidelines

The standard reference for what counts as what in cross-border higher education: branch campuses, franchises, joint degrees, validation. Essential for anyone trying to compare numbers across studies that define TNE differently.

British Council · 2013 · PDF

The Shape of Things to Come: The Evolution of Transnational Education

A landmark study of program and provider mobility with a focus on host countries, written with Jane Knight among others. Older now, but the analytical groundwork for most of what followed.

UNESCO IESALC · 2021 · PDF

Thinking Higher and Beyond: Perspectives on the Futures of Higher Education to 2050

Twenty-five experts from every world region imagine what higher education could become by 2050. The conceptual opening of UNESCO's Futures of Higher Education project, and a rich source of framing for why new institutions get founded.

UNESCO · 2026 · Report page

Transforming Higher Education: Global Collaboration on Visioning and Action

UNESCO's roadmap for higher education, built from the third World Higher Education Conference and consultations with over 15,000 participants. Frames a sector of 269 million students and 22,000+ institutions, with seven guiding principles for its transformation.

UNESCO IESALC · 2026 · Report page

Higher Education Global Trends Report

The first edition of UNESCO's global trends study - a system-level snapshot of inclusion, equity, quality, and mobility across world higher education, linked to the Higher Education Policy Observatory's comparative data.

OBHE & C-BERT · 2016-2017 · Report page

International Branch Campuses: Trends & Developments / Success Factors of Mature IBCs

The two definitive joint reports on the branch campus phenomenon, with full listings of campuses in operation and in development, and interview-based analysis of what makes decade-old campuses succeed. C-BERT's research page collects them alongside two decades of related scholarship.

Universities UK International · annual · Report page

The Scale of UK Higher Education Transnational Education (annual series)

The annual census of UK TNE - over half a million students studying for UK awards in more than 200 countries and territories, broken down by provider, location, and mode. UK TNE enrollment is on track to overtake onshore international recruitment.

NECHE · PDF

Becoming Accredited: A Guide for Institutions Abroad

The pathway document from the most internationally active US accreditor - how a new institution outside the United States earns eligibility, candidacy, and accreditation. Reading it is the fastest way to understand the gate every new US-accredited university abroad must pass through.

World Bank & Mastercard Foundation · 2022- · Report page

Tertiary Education and Skills (TES) Program

The umbrella trust fund now carrying the World Bank's current work on tertiary education and skills, launched with the Mastercard Foundation. Its publications track where new institutional capacity is being financed worldwide.
