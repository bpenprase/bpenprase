Every night this site scans the global higher-education press — from University World News and The PIE News to EducationWorld India and the World Bank — and sorts what it finds into five channels: new startup universities, innovative new programs, branch campuses, innovation in Indian higher education, and important new reports. The goal is a single morning read for anyone researching how, where, and why new universities come into being.
