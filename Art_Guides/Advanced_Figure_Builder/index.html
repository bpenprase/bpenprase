Change pose every

Each new pose starts from one of the presets, sometimes as a mirror image, then gives some of the arms, legs, body and head a new action at random: an arm raised, reaching or on the hip, a step or a lifted knee, a bend or a twist, a turn of the head. The more variation, the more of them change, always within what a body can do. Back returns to earlier poses. The right and left arrow keys work too.

Surface study

Major planes squares each muscle into a few flat facets, so you can see which way each part of the form faces. Contours draws rings around the body and limbs. They rise over every muscle and dip into every groove, showing how the surface turns.

Two values splits the figure into light and shadow only. Three values adds the halftone between them.

Background

With a picture behind it, the view stays level, the way a camera sees a scene. Slide the eye level line onto the horizon in your picture, then set how high your eye is above the floor. The figure casts its shadow onto the picture's floor. Your picture stays on your own device.

The man and woman are built from the open MakeHuman body model (released to the public domain under CC0), shaped toward the eight-heads ideal proportions.
