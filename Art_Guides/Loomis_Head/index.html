Rules built in
  • Side circle is two-thirds the width of the ball. Its cross marks the brow line and the ear line.
  • Hairline, brow, nose sit at the top, middle and bottom of the circle.
  • Chin drops one more of those thirds below the nose.
  • Eyes sit halfway between the crown and the chin, one eye-width apart.
  • Mouth is a third of the way from nose to chin. The ear spans brow to nose.
  • Contours slice the head at even heights, so you can see which way each surface turns. Major planes square the round forms into flat facets in light, halftone and shadow.
  • The four proportion sliders move this head away from those rules. The side circle stays put, so you can see the difference.
