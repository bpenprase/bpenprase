Art Guides

Interactive guides for learning to draw and paint.

Guides

  • Loomis Head Rotator Turn a construction head to any angle, adjust its proportions, and move the light to study the shadows.
  • Portrait Head Mapper Fit the construction head to a photo of a real face, then turn the person to any angle as a reference for a portrait.
  • Figure Builder Build a posable eight-head figure from the pelvis and rib cage up to the basic form and a muscled mannequin.
