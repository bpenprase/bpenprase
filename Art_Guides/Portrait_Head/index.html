Portrait Head Mapper

Fit the construction head to a photo, then turn the person to any angle.

How to use it

  1. Choose a photo of a face looking straight at the camera.
  2. Drag the nine dots onto the pupils, the base of the nose, the line between the lips, the bottom of the chin, the two sides of the face at the cheekbones, and the points where the ear lobes join the head.
  3. The head reshapes to match. Turn it, light it, and fine-tune with the sliders.
