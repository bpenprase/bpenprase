Proportions built in
  • Eight heads tall. Chin at 7, nipples near 6, navel near 5, crotch at 4, bottom of the knees at 2.
  • Man: shoulders 2⅓ heads wide. Woman: shoulders 2 heads, waist 1 head, hips 1½ heads.
  • Arms: elbows at the waist, wrists at the crotch, fingertips at mid-thigh.
  • Realistic mannequin adds simplified muscle groups over the basic form. Switch it off and on to compare the two.
  • Contours are slices taken at even heights while the figure stands. They wrap round each form and follow it as the figure bends, so they show which way every surface turns.
  • Major planes square the round forms into flat facets in three tones, light, halftone and shadow, as a first carving of the surface.
  • Landmarks are the places where bone shows at the surface. Watch how the line through them tilts as the figure bends.
